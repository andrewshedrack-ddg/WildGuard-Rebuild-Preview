Privacy Policy

WildGuard Society collects as little as possible, and this page says plainly what that is.

What is stored on your device

On your own device, the site may store your language and theme choice, your scans, favourites, and history, using your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device unless you send it to us yourself. Clearing your browser data removes it.

What you send us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address, and message you wrote, so we can reply. We do not sell that information.

Payments

No payment is taken on this site yet, so no card or mobile-money details are collected here.

Other sites

Research links open iNaturalist in a new tab. That site has its own privacy policy, and WildGuard does not control it.

Honest limit

The contact form depends on an email service that is not yet fully configured. If a form message does not reach us, email wildguardsociety@gmail.com directly.

Ask us to delete your message

Email wildguardsociety@gmail.com and tell us what to delete, and we will do so.
